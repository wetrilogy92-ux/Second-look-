How do I take a screenshot?
  • iPhone: press the side button and volume up together. On an iPhone with a Home button, press the side button and the Home button.
  • Android: press power and volume down together. A few phones use a different shortcut.
  • Windows: press Windows + Shift + S, drag over the message, then press Ctrl + V here.
  • Mac: press Control + Shift + Command + 4, drag over the message, then press Command + V here.
  • No luck? Take a photo of the screen with another phone or camera.
